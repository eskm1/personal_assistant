# Studio speed on the golden paths, 2026-10-20 (baseline)

The first weekly pass of the usability standard (studio/CLAUDE.md, item 6). Every golden path G1-G5 walked step by step
under Bryan's conditions, timed per step. This is the baseline: next week's pass compares against these numbers.
The friction found on the way (labels, dead ends, extra taps) is in its own list; the worst of it is under "Fix next".

## In short

- **Main screens open within budget once the app has been used.** Plan on Bryan's phone: 1.8-1.9 s to its data
  (budget 2.5 s); on a computer 0.75-1.0 s. Review's Done: 1.0 s phone, 0.7 s desk.
- **The first visit misses.** Michelle's first Plan (cold cache): **4.2 s** and 486 KB in 77 requests.
- **Scheduling is the slowest press.** "Schedule…" takes **3.1-5.3 s** to become usable and "Schedule" up to **5.5 s**,
  because the draft page downloads the whole video in the background. With videos blocked: 0.48 s and 0.41 s.
- **The draft page jumps after it opens** (CLS 0.27 on a phone, budget 0.1): the video tile has no reserved size.
- **Presses mostly answer in time.** 20 misses of the 100 ms, 2.5 s or shift budgets in 82 measured steps; the
  worst press is adding a note (0.3-0.5 s before anything changes on a phone).
- **Not measured yet:** "did anyone use what we built last week?" (usage capture lands with the help and feedback
  work; the first weekly triage answers it).

## Conditions

- **Code:** HEAD c740a10 (a snapshot, so another session's edits in progress don't affect it). It doesn't include
  the help, feedback and usage capture being added on 2026-10-20 (Help on the bar, capture.js): next week's pass
  measures them, including what capture.js adds to every page.
- **Network:** a shaping proxy (scratchpad `golden/shape.mjs`): the Studio machine 200 ms round trip, Supabase 30 ms,
  20 Mbps down / 10 up shared, a TCP handshake per connection, slow start, a 256 KB send buffer. TLS and HTTP/2
  through Caddy, as on the server.
- **Devices:** phone = 390x844, touch, CPU 4x slower (Chromium); desk = 1360x900, CPU 1x.
- **People:** Michelle (team) on a phone, first visit: G1 starts from the Telegram link and signs in, later paths
  reuse that phone. Bryan (owner) on a phone and a desk, daily use: signed in, cache warm, the app opened afresh for
  each path (new connections).
- **Data** (seeded): two client projects with ideas in every group, Topics with 3 suggestions, Everyday posts, an
  archived project; 12 clips; 3 drafts to review (Reel with Studio's read, carousel, Reel), 2 approved, 1
  scheduled, 1 posted; Instagram connected to a local mock. Uploads: an 11 MB, 12 s 1080p clip.
- **Not real:** Make this queues but no editor runs; ingest doesn't run, so an upload ends at "Studio is checking it".
  Videos are VP9 (the sandbox's Chromium has no H.264).
- **Medians of 3 runs**, fresh data each run.

What each column means:

- **Answer**: press to the first change on screen (a pressed state, a spinner or the result, as a change to the page;
  a button's 1 px `:active` nudge isn't counted). Budget 100 ms. For a step that opens the app, the first paint.
- **Next paint**: the browser's own measure of the press (Event Timing, as INP). "<16" is under its floor.
- **Data on screen**: press to the screen showing its data. Budget 2.5 s for a screen.
- **CLS**: layout shift in the step; in brackets, shift after the data was on screen (the standard: none).
- **KB / requests**: on the wire in the step, cache hits excluded; upload bodies and still-playing video not counted.

Why Schedule is slow: opening a draft starts downloading its whole light copy (about 5 MB here, 6 MB for a real 24 s
Reel) although nothing plays, and the Schedule dialog then fetches the full-quality file for the cover picker even
with "First frame" chosen. Its own request (`/api/instagram/plan`, 4 ms on the server) waits behind those bytes on the
phone's one connection. The same steps with videos blocked (`walk.mjs --no-video`): Schedule… 484 ms, Schedule 413 ms.
The harness's buffers may exaggerate the queueing a little; the cause is real on 4G.

## Live site (studio.theurbanmakers.com), read-only

Measured from the sandbox on 2026-10-20 without signing in: no form posts, three runs per page spaced 10 s apart.
Phone (390, touch, CPU 4x), 20/10 Mbps and 40 ms added latency (CDP). The sandbox is not in Singapore and reaches the
server through an agent proxy that ends TLS itself: its time to first byte, 150-210 ms, is the round trip from here
plus the proxy, not Bryan's. Signed-in golden paths stay on the local stack: there is no live QA login yet.

| Page | Cache | TTFB | First paint (= LCP) | Load | CLS | Requests on the wire | KB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| /login | cold | 159-185 ms | 1.16-1.29 s | 1.54-1.82 s | 0 | 10 | 135.5 |
| /login | warm | 159-214 ms | 0.27-0.33 s | 0.25-0.32 s | 0 | 1 | 1.2 |
| /plan/ (signed out: 302 to /login) | cold | 149-171 ms | 1.23-1.57 s | 1.63-2.05 s | 0 | 11 | 135.3 |
| /plan/ | warm | 163-201 ms | 0.41-0.47 s | 0.40-0.46 s | 0 | 2 | 1.2 |

- `/` and `/plan/` signed out answer 302 to `/login?next=/plan/`: the redirect costs 0.69-0.87 s on a new connection,
  0.15-0.17 s warm.
- HTTP/2 through Caddy, HTTP/3 offered (`alt-svc: h3`). HSTS one year.
- Static files are `?v=<hash>` with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`; a warm visit fetches only
  the page. CSS gzipped by the app (theme.css 4.8 KB, shell.css 2.7 KB on the wire); no Brotli; login.js (0.6 KB)
  isn't compressed (too small to matter).
- Of /login's 135 KB, **106 KB are five fonts** (Plex Sans 400/500/600, Plex Mono 400, Barlow 600), and the favicon
  `studio.ico` is **20 KB** uncompressed.
- `/manifest.webmanifest`: 393 bytes, `max-age=3600`.

## Fix next (ranked by what it saves on the golden paths)

1. **No video download before Play** (`preload="none"` and the poster on a draft's tile; the Schedule dialog loads the
   cover video only when "A moment" is picked, from the light copy). G5: Schedule… 3.1-5.3 s and Schedule up to 5.5 s -> about 0.5 s each
   (measured with videos blocked); G1: 5-6 MB less per draft opened on 4G, and every other press on the page stops
   queueing behind it.
2. **Reserve the player's 9:16 box** on a draft's page (`aspect-ratio` on the frame before the video loads). G1 and
   G5: CLS 0.27 -> 0, and Play stops moving under the thumb.
3. **Add a note at once** (clear the box and show the note while it saves; put it back on error). G1: 0.3-0.5 s ->
   under 50 ms, on the most repeated press of the daily path.
4. **First visit to Plan** (Michelle's first week): 4.2 s -> under 2.5 s. Preload the two fonts the first screen
   uses, ask Studio for the planner session in parallel with the modules (not after), and load the modules the first
   screen doesn't need (refs, library, shoot, slides, month) after it paints.
5. **"Sign in" answers at once** ("Signing in…" on the button): 1.4 s of nothing on Michelle's first press.
6. **Ideas opens on the project that has work** (next site day, else latest activity) and the "new ideas" banner gets
   its space before the cards. G2/G3: 2 taps and about 1 s less for a first-time user, CLS 0.096 -> 0.
7. **Sheets and dialogs open their frame first** (+ New idea 131-158 ms, Play 122-134 ms, Go on a suggestion 127 ms on
   a phone): draw the sheet empty in the same frame, fill it after. All just over 100 ms at CPU 4x.
8. **The toast floats** (position fixed, outside the tile column): Send back's CLS 0.092 -> 0.
